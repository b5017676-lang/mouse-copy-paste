# Privacy Policy / מדיניות פרטיות

Extension: העתקה והדבקה בעכבר (Mouse Copy & Paste)
Last updated: 2026-10-02

## English
This extension does not collect, store, share, sell or transmit any personal data.
When you click the toolbar icon, the extension pastes your clipboard content into the page you are on (Google Docs or Google Sheets), or copies your selection when you choose "Copy" from the icon's menu. Clipboard content is handled only in your browser, only at that moment, and is not saved or sent anywhere.
The extension uses Chrome's debugger interface only to send a Ctrl+V or Ctrl+C key press to the active tab. It does not read page content, browsing history or any other data.
The extension has no servers, analytics or third-party services.
